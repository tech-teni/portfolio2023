About

I am a senior frontend developer with over six years of experience building responsive, accessible, data-heavy web applications. For the last three years I have worked in health information systems, leading frontend modules on LAMISPlus, an electronic medical records platform used across more than 180 facilities. I specialise in React and TypeScript, data visualization and performance at scale, and I also work across the stack with Spring Boot and SQL. I hold an MSc in Information Management, and I care about building technology that is fast, reliable and genuinely useful to the people who depend on it.

Outside health, I've led frontend work across advertising, social, education and commerce: 100,000+ users at Admoni, a 10-person team at Calmglobal, a report-card system used by 500+ schools, and a legacy-to-TypeScript migration at Grapevine.
