01 / About

I turn complex, high-stakes systems into interfaces that people actually trust.

“I care about building technology that is fast, reliable, and genuinely useful to the people who depend on it.”

I'm a front-end engineer with six years of experience building responsive, accessible, data-heavy web applications in React and TypeScript. For the last three years I've worked in health information systems, building front-end modules on LAMISPlus, an electronic medical records platform used across 180+ facilities and holding over 2 million patient records. I build component-driven UI using Atomic Design principles, and I've built and maintained a design system adopted across 10+ engineering teams.

My work spans complex state management, highly optimized rendering for large datasets, WCAG-conscious accessibility, and front-end security and I'm equally comfortable reasoning about the data my interfaces depend on, with two years of backend work in Spring Boot and SQL. I hold an MSc in Information Management from Ahmadu Bello University.

Outside health, I've led front-end work across advertising, social, education and commerce: 100,000+ users at Admoni, a 10-person team at Calmglobal, a report-card system used by 500+ schools, and a legacy-to-TypeScript migration at Grapevine.
